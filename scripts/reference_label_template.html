How to judge. Look at the yellow-outlined field across the three dates. In SWIR false colour fresh burn scars turn near black or deep maroon; in true colour they look charcoal grey or black and are usually sharply bounded by the field edge. Tilled soil is brown-grey to pink-purple in SWIR, not black; freshly irrigated soil is dark in true colour but stays reddish-purple in SWIR. Partial burns count as Burnt. Use Can't tell for haze, cloud or ambiguous cases. The detector's answer is hidden.
