Burn label review

How to judge. Look at the yellow-outlined field across the three dates. In the SWIR view, fresh burn scars turn very dark (near black / deep maroon) and in true colour they look charcoal-grey or black, usually sharply bounded by the field edge. Tilled soil is brown-grey to pink-purple in SWIR, but not black; wet freshly irrigated soil is dark in true colour and stays reddish-purple in SWIR. Partial burns count as Burnt. Use Can't tell for haze, cloud or ambiguous cases. The detector's answer is hidden.
