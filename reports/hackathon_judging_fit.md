# Greenovators Hackathon 2026: judging fit

## What the brochure says

The event theme combines “Waste to Wealth,” sustainable infrastructure, and net-zero AI. Its objectives include circular economy, practical feasibility, scalability, and environmental impact. The brochure calls it a 30-hour hackathon, but the printed schedule runs from Wednesday, 7 October at 2:00 PM to Thursday, 8 October at 6:00 PM (28 hours). Project submission is listed for 5:00 PM on 8 October; team pitches are Friday, 9 October from 9:00 AM to noon.

The listed Track 3 panel includes Dr. Alok Bhushan Mukherjee, a remote-sensing/GIS analyst and R&D head, alongside Dr. Ashish Mani, Professor at AUUP. Track 1 includes a production/R&D head; Track 2 includes a Central Water Commission scientist. The brochure does not show a weighted scoring rubric.

## Why this project fits

PARALI SE PAISA connects satellite observations of paddy fields to a possible circular use for post-harvest straw. The geospatial monitoring work speaks directly to the Track 3 expertise; the biomass collection and buyer workflow support the Waste to Wealth theme. The product must show where evidence comes from and how a field-status candidate would be checked before anyone acts on it.

## Honest demo sequence

1. In `DATA_MODE=real`, show the Muktsar field map, Sentinel-2 observation history, derived indices, weather provenance, and evidence-quality notice. The current dataset has 520 observed research fields and 3,640 field-date rows over seven acquisition dates. These polygons are research boundaries, not cadastral parcels.
2. Explain that the app has no trusted status model or independent field-status ground truth. Weak proxy labels are exploratory; FIRMS credentials and field-level Sentinel-1 observations are absent. Straw tonnage and operational eligibility are therefore withheld in real mode.
3. Switch to the synthetic demo to show assignment, collection, buyer matching, and evidence-verification workflow. Call those records and outcomes synthetic; they demonstrate the intended product flow, not existing partnerships or measured field impact.

## Pitch line

“We are building a traceable satellite-to-market workflow for paddy straw: observe fields, surface reviewable harvest candidates, then connect verified residue to a resource buyer. Today we demonstrate real Muktsar satellite evidence and a separate synthetic operations workflow; a field pilot will supply the independent labels, yield measurements, and buyer data needed to validate estimates and quantify impact.”

Do not claim winning is guaranteed. The brochure’s broad themes and named panel indicate fit, but the judging weights and competing submissions are not provided.
