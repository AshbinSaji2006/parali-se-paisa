## Real-data evidence

- The analysis contains 74 Sentinel-2 acquisitions from 2023–2026 and 12.6 million field-date observations over model-derived Fields of The World boundaries.
- All optical burn outputs are **RULE-BASED BURN CANDIDATES**: **STRICT_BURN_CANDIDATE** and **LOOSE_BURN_CANDIDATE**. They are not independently validated burn labels. Human review labels completed: 0.
- Loose candidate area from non-overlapping 20 m pixels: **21,121 ha (2023), 10,566 ha (2024), 22,941 ha (2025)**. This is an exploratory rule-derived series, not official burned area.
- Strict candidate area: **649 ha (2023), 1,022 ha (2024), 2,269 ha (2025)**. Do not present as a year-to-year trend: haze and cloud change clean pre-event coverage.
- In 2025, **8.8%** of strict optical candidates had a matched VIIRS active-fire detection within the defined matching window (500 m; ≤5-day evidence window). Say: “About 9 in 10 strict optical burn candidates had no matched VIIRS active-fire detection within the defined matching window.” Do not describe this as a satellite miss rate. FIRMS/VIIRS is corroborative thermal context, not ground truth.
- Burn risk is a normalized score, not probability. Replay comparisons are counterfactual analyses under stated assumptions, not measured operational impacts.

## Action workflow boundaries

- Research Evidence is a read-only view of real satellite observations.
- Baler dispatch and buyer workflows use **DEMO/SYNTHETIC** records. Routes are straight-line **GEODESIC_PROXY**, not road travel-time routing.
- A certificate is a **PROTOTYPE QR metadata check**. It is not a government certificate, an independently established burn-free result, or incentive approval.
- The 12.1 lakh tonne straw figure was reported by **The Tribune**, as cited in reference 11 of the concept note. It is a news-reported estimate, not an official statistic.
