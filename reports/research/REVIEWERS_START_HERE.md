# Reviewers start here

Use only these blinded review pages:

- [2023 blinded human-review workflow](label_tool/label_reference_2023.html)
- [2025 blinded human-review workflow](label_tool/label_reference_2025.html)

Make an independent BURNED / NOT_BURNED / UNCERTAIN label before opening any supplemental context. The page does not show the detector tier, detector decision, or rule score before the independent label.

Do not open detector strata, chips, sample-design files, legacy labeling pages, or internal research material before labeling. Those materials are in internal_do_not_share and are not part of the reviewer workflow.
